About

Hi! I’m Zimeng Lu, a second-year Biomedical Informatics student in the dual-degree programme at Zhejiang University and The University of Edinburgh. I’m interested in applying AI and computational methods to biological and biomedical problems, particularly multi-omics, biomedical imaging, and drug discovery.

I currently lead two projects in Chaochen Wang’s lab at Zhejiang University: multiSAID, a cross-species multi-omics platform for human and mouse skin diseases, and HF-OrganoidNet, an AI-assisted hair follicle organoid drug-screening project. My experience spans computational modelling, bioinformatics, and wet-lab research.
