About

Hi, I’m Katie Lu. I study at the Zhejiang University–University of Edinburgh (ZJE) Institute. I’m curious about how biology and data can come together to help us understand living systems.

I enjoy exploring how computational tools can help us ask better biological questions. This website is a place to share what I’m learning, the projects I’m working on, and the ideas I’d like to explore next.

Beyond studying, I love chocolate and listening to Westlife, Backstreet Boys, and A1. This is also a small corner of the internet for the things that make me curious—and happy.
